# Generate Paid Traffic Assets

Transform the transcript into an evidence-based performance marketing package.

## Workflow

1. Obtain the offer, audience, campaign objective and target platform. Infer missing audience details only as labeled hypotheses.
2. Extract the central promise, mechanisms, pains, desires, objections, proof and usable statements from the transcript.
3. Separate transcript-supported facts from strategic hypotheses. Never invent metrics, testimonials, guarantees or product attributes.
4. Produce the package in Brazilian Portuguese with copy-ready sections.
5. Flag claims, compliance risks and details requiring human validation.

## Required Package

- Strategic summary, central promise and awareness stage.
- Avatar: pains, desires, objections and buying triggers.
- Ten video titles and ten ad headlines.
- Complete video description with CTA and keywords.
- Seven hooks for the first three seconds.
- Five creative angles with visual concepts and 30–60 second scripts.
- Five primary ad texts in short and long versions.
- CTAs and truthful thumbnail concepts.
- Cold, warm and remarketing campaign structure.
- Audience, exclusion and placement hypotheses.
- Prioritized A/B plan with variable, hypothesis and decision criterion.
- Funnel metrics and compliance review.

## Quality Rules

- Match the platform, objective and audience instead of producing generic copy.
- Preserve names, prices and claims exactly when supported.
- Avoid promises of guaranteed results and misleading urgency.
- Make each creative angle materially distinct.
- Deliver concise headings, scannable lists and blocks ready to copy.
